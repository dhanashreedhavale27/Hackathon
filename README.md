Campus Flow by Christ

Modern academic institutions face a persistent coordination crisis. Universities generate a constant stream of 
time-bound activities;cultural fests, technical symposiums, departmental events, workshops, competitions, and 
student initiatives. Yet the infrastructure to support them is fundamentally broken.

Designing and building a modular, theme-agnostic platform that enables decentralized groups (departments, clubs, 
committees) to publish, promote, and manage time-sensitive activities;while allowing users to seamlessly 
discover, engage with, and participate in opportunities;all without relying on fragmented channels or rebuilding 
systems from scratch.
